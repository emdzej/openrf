# Playing

## The objective

Each side has a **home bunker** with a lift pad and a **flag hidden in one of its flag
buildings**. To win, find the enemy flag, bring it back to your own pad — **in the jeep**,
the only vehicle that can carry it.

1. Scout and blast enemy flag buildings until the flag appears (you'll hear the
   *Flag Discovery* theme).
2. Clear a path, then drive a jeep over the flag to pick it up.
3. Bring it home to your pad. The jeep is fragile: if it is destroyed the flag drops where it was.

You lose when you run out of jeeps.

## Starting a game

- Watch or skip the intro (any key), then press **F2** on the title screen to start the current
  level, or **1–9** to start the first map of that difficulty level.
- You start inside the **bunker lift**: pick a vehicle with **W/A/S/D** and launch it with
  **H**. The number shows how many of that vehicle you have left.
- Drive back onto your pad and press **H** to dock: the vehicle is returned to your stock and
  you can choose another one.

## The dashboard

The status bar shows the **radar** (centred on your vehicle; your pad, known flag sites and
vehicles are marked), the **fuel** gauge (blinks when low — refuel at a fuel depot or by
docking), and your **ammo**. Drive over an ammo dump to resupply.

## Things that will hurt you

- **Gun towers** wake up when you come into view and track you. Destroy them with the tank.
- **Drones** hunt any vehicle that stays in one place too long (from level 4 onwards).
- **Mines** are scattered on later levels.
- **Deep water** sinks everything except the helicopter and the jeep in boat mode.
- Fly the helicopter off the edge of the map and a **submarine** will come for you.

## Two players

Press **F3** on the title screen. The screen splits: player 1 (left) uses the keyboard's
letter keys, player 2 (right) the numeric keypad — see [Controls](./controls). Each side has
its own bunker, stock and flag; the first to bring the other's flag home wins. A player who
runs out of vehicles watches from the bunker until the game ends. Sound is split too: player 1's
world on the left speaker, player 2's on the right.

## Levels

The Windows edition ships 100 one-player maps across 9 difficulty levels (and 104 two-player
maps). After a win OpenRF starts the next map. See [Choose a level](/howto/choose-level).
